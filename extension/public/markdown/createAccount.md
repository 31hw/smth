Creating an account on Zekoro now happens directly from the front page.

## What you need

* A Discord account
* Access to the Discord account you want linked
* A unique username and password for Zekoro

## How sign up works

1. Go to the [home page](/)
2. Click **Verify with Discord**
3. Finish the Discord authorization flow
4. Return to Zekoro and choose your username and password
5. Click **Sign Up**

Your account is created instantly after Discord verification. There is no separate application review page anymore.

## Important notes

* One Discord account can only be linked to one Zekoro account
* Your signup is logged to Discord automatically
* If your Discord account is already linked, use your existing account to log in instead of signing up again
